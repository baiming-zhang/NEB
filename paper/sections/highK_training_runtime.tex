\paragraph{Measured short run and cost estimates.}
The completed $K=1024$ Potential endpoint contains 25 optimizer updates and no scheduled validation; its 13267.46-second elapsed interval includes failed attempts, repairs, waits and atomic publication of the full model/Adam state. This short-run elapsed interval is separate from the projected full-training cost in Table~\ref{tab:Pot-training-basis-cost}.

The $K=1024$ Potential training estimate uses 22 measured updates (epochs 3--24; mean 430.68 seconds) and 166 ordinary updates from the completed $K=512$ run (mean 250.78 seconds). Their ratio scales the measured $K=512$ 2000-update total of 504713.77 seconds, retaining its validation and checkpoint allowance. The resulting estimate is about 866800 seconds (240.8 hours); validation and checkpoint costs are assumed to scale by the same factor as the update workload. Potential-AL training costs are extrapolated from measured $K=32,64,128$ runs and matched Potential-FG costs from $K=32,64,128,256$ runs using a least-squares power law in $K$. Basis-generation estimates use an affine fit in $K$ to the measured lower-capacity full-point intervals for the same method. Estimated training and basis times are rounded to 100 and 10 seconds, respectively. These projections assume the same implementation, hardware, sampling and output boundary, with no recovery event. They do not imply trained high-capacity checkpoints or measured reconstruction accuracy.
